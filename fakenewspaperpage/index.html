Local Student Builds 200 Websites in 10 Days

By Staff Reporter | Technology

In a remarkable feat of dedication and hard work, Ansari Mohammed Faisal, a third year B.Tech student from MIT College Aurangabad, has embarked on an ambitious challenge to build 200 websites in just 10 days. The young developer, who is specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, says he took up the challenge to improve his web development skills and build a strong portfolio.

"I want to show that with consistency and hard work, anything is possible," said Faisal. "Each website teaches me something new." His project has already attracted attention from students and faculty alike at MIT College.
