🎬 Movie Reviews

My honest reviews of movies I've watched

🚀

Interstellar (2014)

Director: Christopher Nolan | Sci-Fi
★★★★★

One of the greatest sci-fi films ever made. The concept of time dilation and black holes is mind-bending. Hans Zimmer's soundtrack is absolutely beautiful. A must watch!

🎓

3 Idiots (2009)

Director: Rajkumar Hirani | Comedy Drama
★★★★★

The most relatable movie for any Indian student. Funny, emotional and inspiring at the same time. The message about following your passion over grades is timeless.

💎

KGF Chapter 2 (2022)

Director: Prashanth Neel | Action
★★★★☆

Yash's performance is simply outstanding. The action sequences are epic and the background score is goosebump-worthy. One of the best Indian action films in recent times.
