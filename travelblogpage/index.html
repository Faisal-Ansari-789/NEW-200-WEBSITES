🕌 Ajanta & Ellora Caves — Aurangabad

📅 December 2025 | 📍 Aurangabad, Maharashtra

The Ajanta and Ellora caves are a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of India's greatest treasures. The rock-cut architecture and ancient paintings left me speechless. A must-visit for anyone who loves history.

🌊 Tarkarli Beach — Sindhudurg

📅 January 2026 | 📍 Sindhudurg, Maharashtra

Crystal clear blue water, white sand beaches and amazing seafood. Tarkarli is one of Maharashtra's hidden gems. The snorkelling experience here was absolutely unforgettable!

Beach

🏔️ Lonavala Hill Station

📅 February 2026 | 📍 Pune, Maharashtra

Perfect weekend getaway from Pune. Green valleys, misty mountains and famous chikki! The view from Tiger's Leap point is breathtaking especially during monsoon season.
