🍛 Chicken Biryani

A classic Indian rice dish full of flavour and spices

⏱️

Prep: 30 min

🔥

Cook: 45 min

🍽️

Serves: 4

⭐

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • Biryani masala, salt, oil
  • Fresh mint and coriander
  • Saffron soaked in warm milk

Steps

  1. Wash and soak rice for 30 minutes
  2. Fry onions until golden brown
  3. Add chicken, tomatoes, yogurt and spices
  4. Cook chicken until tender
  5. Boil rice until 70% done
  6. Layer rice over chicken in pot
  7. Pour saffron milk on top
  8. Cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes
  9. Mix gently and serve hot!
