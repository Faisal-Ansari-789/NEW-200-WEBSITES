📚 My Book Reviews

Books I've read and loved

📖

Atomic Habits

by James Clear
★★★★★
A life-changing book about how small habits compound over time. If you want to build good habits and break bad ones, this is the book to read. Highly recommend to every student!
📖

The Alchemist

by Paulo Coelho
★★★★★
An inspiring story about following your dreams and listening to your heart. Simple language but very deep meaning. I finished it in one sitting!
📖

Rich Dad Poor Dad

by Robert Kiyosaki
★★★★☆
Changed the way I think about money and investing. Every young person should read this book before they start earning. Real eye-opener!
